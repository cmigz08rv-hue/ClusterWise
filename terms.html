The short version: ClusterWise is a free guide to help you think about your Senior High School strand. It is not a test you pass or fail, and it does not decide your strand. Use it honestly, and talk through your results with people who know you.

1. About ClusterWise

PCSHS ClusterWise is a free web tool made by a student team of Pasay City South High School (PCSHS). By using the site you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use it.

2. Who it is for

The assessment is for learners in Grade 10 and below who are choosing a strand. The grade check is self-declared, so please answer it honestly. Strand pages and the FAQ can be read by anyone.

3. Results are guidance, not a decision

  • Results show where your skills and interests stand today, based on a short test and a short questionnaire. They are not a prediction of your future and not a verdict on what you can become.
  • The questions and score thresholds were written by our team, are not a standardized or published instrument, and are still being reviewed. Treat the results as one input among many.
  • ClusterWise is not an official PCSHS placement, admission or enrollment tool. Your strand choice is not made, approved or changed by this site.
  • PCSHS does not allow learners to switch strands once chosen, so please talk with your guidance counselor, teachers and family before you decide.

4. Using the site fairly

  • Answer on your own, without searching, asking others or using AI, so the results reflect you.
  • Don't try to break, overload, scrape or tamper with the site, or copy it and present it as your own.
  • Don't use the site to harass or mislead anyone, for example by sharing altered results.

5. Content and ownership

The site's design, questions, text and code were made by the ClusterWise team for PCSHS. You may save your own results picture and share it for personal use. Embedded YouTube videos and other third-party material belong to their owners and are subject to their terms.

6. Privacy

How your information is handled is explained in our Privacy Policy. In short, your answers stay in your browser and clear when you close the tab.

7. No guarantees

ClusterWise is provided "as is" for learner guidance.
