Discover Your Ideal Senior High Strand here in PCSHS

PCSHS ClusterWise is a free online tool that helps learners of Pasay City South High School (PCSHS) choose a Senior High School strand. Take six short skill tests, one for each strand, plus a quick questionnaire about what you enjoy. ClusterWise shows your skills and your interests side by side, so you can see where they line up.

How it works

  1. Click Start Assessment and confirm that you are in Grade 10 or below. The Assessment Hub then opens.
  2. The hub has six skill tests, one per strand, with 15 multiple-choice questions each (90 in total), plus an Interests questionnaire (30 questions, no right or wrong answers). Take them in any order.
  3. Every skill question has one correct answer. Answers are not revealed and a finished part cannot be retaken, so take your time.
  4. Once all seven parts are done, See Overall Results unlocks.
  5. Your results show your skill score and interest ranking for every strand, one main takeaway from comparing them, and a breakdown of your strengths. Then explore that strand's subjects, skills, and career paths.

Your strand shapes your next two years

You choose a strand once, then stay in it for all of Grade 11 and Grade 12. PCSHS does not allow learners to switch strands, so there is no second try.

A strand that does not fit your skills and interests can make those two years harder than they need to be, and it can hold you back from what you are capable of. Check where you stand before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how ClusterWise works and what happens to your answers.

What is PCSHS ClusterWise?+

PCSHS ClusterWise is a free strand-matching tool for Junior High School learners here in Pasay City South High School. No account is needed. Six skill tests (one per strand) and an interests questionnaire show where your skills and interests line up, so you can see which strand fits you.

Who is it for?+

ClusterWise is made for Junior High School learners at Pasay City South High School (PCSHS), ideally Grade 10, who are deciding which Senior High School strand to take. When you press Start Assessment, you are asked which grade you are in, because the assessment is only for learners in Grade 10 and below. If you choose Grade 11–12, the assessment stays locked in that browser tab.

Can I change my strand later?+

No. PCSHS does not allow learners to switch strands. The strand you choose is the one you stay in for Grade 11 and Grade 12, so compare your skills and interests before you decide, and talk with your guidance counselor if you are unsure.

How long does it take?+

There are 90 skill questions (15 per strand) and 30 interest questions, split into seven short parts. Take them in any order, and do one part at a time if you like. Most learners finish in about 45 minutes.

Can I stop and continue later?+

Yes, as long as you stay in the same browser tab. Your progress is saved on your device and picks up where you left off, even after a refresh. Closing the tab clears it.

Are my answers saved or shared?+

No. ClusterWise has no server or database, so nothing is uploaded or shared. Your answers stay on your device. If you use Save my results, the picture is made on your device too.

Why are skills and interests shown separately?+

They answer different questions: what you can do right now, and what you enjoy. Mixing them into one score would hide that. Side by side, you can see where they agree and where they point different ways.

Is the result final?+

No. ClusterWise is a guide, not a decision. Use your results together with advice from your teachers, guidance counselor, and family.
