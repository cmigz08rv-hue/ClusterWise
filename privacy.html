Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

The short version: ClusterWise has no accounts, no server and no database. We don't ask for your name or email. Your answers and results stay in your own browser and disappear when you close the tab. A few outside services (hosting, fonts and YouTube videos) may see basic technical information, explained below.

Who we are

PCSHS ClusterWise is a free strand-exploration tool for Junior High School learners of Pasay City South High School (PCSHS). It was built as a student project by the Senior High School TechPro Track team. It is a guide for learners, not an official school system.

What we collect

We do not collect, receive or store your personal information. ClusterWise does not ask for your name, email, LRN, section or any account details, and nothing you answer is sent to us.

What stays on your device

While you use the site, your browser keeps the following in session storage, on your device only:

  • your answers to the skill tests and the Interests questionnaire, and your scores;
  • the grade you picked on the grade check;
  • your language and light/dark mode choices;
  • where you are in the assessment, and small flags such as "pledge ticked" or "notice already seen".

This is cleared when you close the tab. It is not shared with anyone. You can also clear it yourself through your browser's site-data settings.

Your results picture

The "Save my results" picture is created on your own device and nothing is uploaded. Once you download or share it, it is yours. If you send it to someone, that is your choice, so share it only with people you trust, such as your family, teachers or guidance counselor.

Outside services

ClusterWise itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising tools. However, the site relies on three outside services, and your browser contacts them directly:

  • Vercel (hosting). Our site is delivered as static files from Vercel. Like most web hosts, Vercel may record technical details of each visit, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in its server logs. We do not use these logs to identify or track learners.
  • Google Fonts. The site's fonts are loaded from Google, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.
  • YouTube. Strand pages that have a recap video show an embedded YouTube player. When such a page loads, YouTube (Google) may collect information and set cookies under its own policies, even if you don't press play. This only happens on those strand pages. If you prefer to avoid it, you can skip the strand videos.

We don't control these services. Please see their own privacy policies for how they handle data.

Learners and minors

ClusterWise is made for Junior High School learners. Because it collects no personal information, it asks nothing that identifies a learner. The grade check is self-declared and used only to decide which screens to show. We encourage learners to discuss their results with parents or guardians, teachers and the guidance counselor.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your session data is under your control, and closing the tab removes it. This approach follows the intent of the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) to keep personal data handling to a minimum.

Changes to this policy

If ClusterWise changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, adding analytics), we will update this page and the date above first.
